# Proposal-to-code coverage

The authoritative source is [the unmodified 23-page proposal](proposal/chatgpt-pro-research-methodology.pdf), dated 21 October 2026. Its exact text is also retained as [extracted-text.txt](proposal/extracted-text.txt). The PDF is a proposed experiment, not a report of trained-model results. `protocol_fingerprint.json` records its SHA-256 and the frozen resolved configuration.

| Proposal section | Implementation | Saved evidence / checks |

The source proposal does not specify every engineering choice. Nothing unspecified is silently presented as a result from the PDF; the decisions document lists those choices and small ambiguities.
